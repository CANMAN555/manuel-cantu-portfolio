I'm a coordinator. I take a lot of moving parts, decide what comes first, get the right people lined up, keep costs in check, and keep everyone in the loop until the job is done. I've done this as a fleet repair coordinator, as a service manager leading a team of 12, and on my own technology projects.

My skills cover a wide range: scheduling, vendor management, team leadership, training, customer service, sales goals, and inventory. I started out as a factory-certified diesel mechanic, so I know hands-on work too. I'm also good with technology: I've worked as an AI trainer, and I planned and launched a free website that helps drivers and dispatchers find truck repair shops, built with AI tools and GitHub. I speak English and Spanish.

My Mission

Five steps I follow on every job, from the first call to the final invoice.

  1. 01

    Prioritize

    Handle several breakdowns at once and decide what gets fixed first, based on safety and what the operation needs.

  2. 02

    Align

    Keep drivers, mechanics, dealerships, and vendors on the same page from the first call.

  3. 03

    Create

    Set up the repair plan and schedule, and build tools that help, like my OpenRoad Service Map website.

  4. 04

    Dispatch

    Find and send repair shops, mobile mechanics, and tow trucks anywhere in the country.

  5. 05

    Close the loop

    Check estimates and invoices, keep everyone updated until the truck is back out, and keep the records up to date.
