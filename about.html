About Me

A coordinator with a wide range of skills, from leading people to building technology to hands-on mechanical work.

I'm the person who keeps the job organized. When there's a lot going on, I figure out what needs to happen first, get the right people on it, watch the costs, and make sure everyone knows what's going on until it's finished. I've done that as a fleet repair coordinator, as a service manager running a team of 20, and on my own tech projects.

I've picked up a lot of different skills along the way: scheduling, working with vendors, leading and training a team, taking care of customers, hitting sales goals, and keeping inventory under control. I got my start as a factory-certified diesel mechanic, so I'm not afraid to get my hands dirty. I'm also big on technology. I've worked as an AI trainer, and I planned and launched a free website, built with AI tools and GitHub, that helps drivers and dispatchers find truck repair shops. I'm Colombian American, and I read, speak, and write both English and Spanish.

My Mission

Five steps I follow on every job, from the first call to the final invoice.

  1. 01

    Prioritize

    Handle several breakdowns at once and decide what gets fixed first, based on safety and what the operation needs.

  2. 02

    Align

    Keep drivers, mechanics, dealerships, and vendors on the same page from the first call.

  3. 03

    Create

    Set up the repair plan and schedule, and build tools that help, like my OpenRoad Service Map website.

  4. 04

    Dispatch

    Find and send repair shops, mobile mechanics, and tow trucks anywhere in the country.

  5. 05

    Close the loop

    Check estimates and invoices, keep everyone updated until the truck is back out, and keep the records up to date.
